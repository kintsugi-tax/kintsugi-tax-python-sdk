# FloridaFields

State-specific fields for Florida (eFile & Pay) registration import.

Florida requires no 2FA/MFA setup, so there is no ``mfa_completed`` field, and
the portal grants no customer-facing third-party access step, so there is no
``third_party_access_enabled``


## Fields

| Field                                                                                                                                                                | Type                                                                                                                                                                 | Required                                                                                                                                                             | Description                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `registration_type`                                                                                                                                                  | *Literal["SALES_TAX"]*                                                                                                                                               | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                   | Registration type for this Florida import: sales tax.                                                                                                                |
| `business_name`                                                                                                                                                      | *str*                                                                                                                                                                | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                   | State-registered business name as shown on the Florida DOR account.                                                                                                  |
| `fl_certificate_number`                                                                                                                                              | *str*                                                                                                                                                                | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                   | Florida Certificate of Registration number (Sales Tax Certificate Number) issued by the Florida Department of Revenue. Also stored as the registration Sales Tax ID. |
| `business_partner_number`                                                                                                                                            | *str*                                                                                                                                                                | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                   | Florida Business Partner Number: a 7- or 10-digit number assigned by the state. 7-digit values are normalized to 10 digits by prepending three zeros.                |
